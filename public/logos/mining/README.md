## Brand context

ViaBTC publishes the selected mark under Brand Images & Colors Guidelines; F2Pool publishes the selected symbol in its downloadable Media Kit. Foundry, Luxor and AntPool publish the selected favicons on their official pool websites. Kryptex publishes the selected symbol on its Brand Assets page; Sluicey uses the selected mark on its official pool homepage. The inspected links did not state a separate asset reuse license. These are the original providers' brand marks, retaining their published colors and backgrounds.

Kryptex's brand page prohibits stretching, recoloring, adding or removing parts, and adding visual effects to the mark. It asks for clear space equal to the symbol's height. Preserve the downloaded image and its natural aspect ratio when displaying it.

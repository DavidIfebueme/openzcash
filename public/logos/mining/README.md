## Brand context

ViaBTC publishes the selected mark under Brand Images & Colors Guidelines; F2Pool publishes the selected symbol in its downloadable Media Kit. Foundry and AntPool publish the selected favicons on their official pool websites. Luxor publishes the selected original PNG in its official Logos & Branding downloads, with Golden Yellow specified as #FFC547. Kryptex publishes the selected symbol on its Brand Assets page; Sluicey uses the selected mark on its official pool homepage. Luxor's brand page states that its graphics are proprietary and protected under intellectual property laws. The inspected links did not state a separate asset reuse license. These are the original providers' brand marks, retaining their published colors and backgrounds.

Kryptex's brand page prohibits stretching, recoloring, adding or removing parts, and adding visual effects to the mark. It asks for clear space equal to the symbol's height. Preserve the downloaded image and its natural aspect ratio when displaying it.

## Display on dark surfaces

All seven shipped marks are original PNG files; none were rasterized from SVG or ICO. Use contain scaling and retain natural aspect ratios, colors and backgrounds. A universal white badge is unnecessary for ViaBTC, F2Pool, AntPool, Kryptex and Luxor. Foundry retains its opaque black tile. Sluicey retains its opaque dark green tile; round the displayed image to conceal its outer white corner pixels. Do not invert, recolor or apply brand-changing filters.

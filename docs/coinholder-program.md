# Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program (`/coinholders`)

The program has its own app in the sidebar, directly above ZCG, labelled **Coinholder Grants** with the official name, _Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program_, as its tooltip and as the Overview's title (the breadcrumb names the program on the other pages). The Financial Privacy Foundation appears only as the program's administrator (its app is "FPF · Program administrator"), as the program proposal describes it: FPF runs submissions, review summaries, KYC and reporting; coinholders decide; the key-holders pay and can veto (ZIP 1016).

## Pages

The sidebar lists Program overview, Results, Voting activity, Verify it yourself, The fund and Coinholder Copilot, in that order. The app opens Program overview at `/coinholders`. Voting activity at `/coinholders/activity` contains the five Q3 2026 charts and a shared proposal selector; `/coinholders/overview` redirects to the program landing page. See [Voting activity and analytics](coinholder-analytics.md) for the source, validation and unit boundaries.

Old addresses redirect permanently: `/zcg/coinholder` and `/coinholder` to the program landing page, `/zcg/coinholder/votes` and `/coinholders/votes` to `?round=all`, `/coinholders/<round>` to `/coinholders/results?round=<round>`, and `/zcg/coinholder/pool` to the fund (FPF's Q3 review post links to `/zcg/coinholder`).

Participation is the largest single vote of a round (the most ZEC cast on any one proposal or poll question), over the 21,000,000 ZEC supply (the ZIP 1016 minimum of 420,000 ZEC is 2%).

**Payouts.** `PAYOUTS` in `program.ts` records each round's payout as FPF reports it: date, the grants' USD value, the ZEC sent and FPF's announcement. `payoutSummary()` in `fund.ts` turns it into the fund page's "Paid from the fund" panel (`fund/payouts.tsx`): one row per paid round with the ZEC/USD price the two figures imply, totals, and the approved round still pending, if any. While a round is pending the page also shows what its grants would take of the balance at today's price; once FPF reports it paid that panel goes, and the sheet's balance already nets the payout out. Q3 2026 was paid on October 16, 2026: 7,320.535 ZEC for the 17 grants ($8,389,992 at $1,146.09 per ZEC), sent from the multisig through the shielded pool in three transactions; FPF confirmed on October 19 (thread 57909, post 23). Each funded Q3 proposal carries its ZEC paid as a note, from the sheet's "ZEC Disbursed" column.

## Data

Everything is curated in `src/lib/coinholder/rounds.ts` (rounds, newest first) and `src/lib/coinholder/program.ts` (rules, key-holders, FPF's role, next round), with the source of each figure beside it:

- **Outcomes**: each round's official results post. Q3 2026 uses FPF's post as edited on 2026-09-30, when Connaugh Zcash Videos moved from Abstain to Funded after str4d pointed out that a simple majority under ZIP 1016 leaves abstentions out (17 funded, 20 rejected).
- **Q3 2026 tallies**: the finalized round `2cba66eb…5e01` on the Valar Group vote chain (`/shielded-vote/v1/vote-summary/<round>`; values are in units of 0.125 ZEC, divide by 8). The Valar, Zcash Foundation, ZODL and Shielded Labs vote servers return byte-identical copies. The figures match, to the cent, the table T. S. Felstead (VASP Holdings) transcribed from the Vizor wallet (forum thread 57910) and every leading answer, share and ZEC total on zakura.com/voting.
- **Q1 2026 tallies**: the Voting Authorities' recount with the published election seed (thread 55048, post 17). The seventh question, on rescinding the Q4 2025 ECC grant, was discarded for skipping the review period; its votes were never published.
- **Q4 2025 tallies**: FPF's results summary (thread 53487). No Abstain option; one transparent voter's 105,002 ZEC is in every total. The ECC grant passed and was later vetoed by Shielded Labs and the Zcash Foundation.
- **Ratification (September 2025)**: thread 52013, post 38, and the Shielded Labs summary.
- **Payouts**: FPF's Coinholder Grants tab of the ZCG spreadsheet (`Paid Out`, `ZEC Disbursed`, `ZEC/USD`; `COINHOLDER_SHEET_URL`) and FPF's forum confirmation of each round. The key-holders' multisig `t3ev37Q2uL1sfTsiJQJiWJoFzQpDhmnUwYo` (ZIP 271; `FUND_MULTISIG`) shows the same totals leaving on-chain: 436.77 ZEC on February 3, 2026, 129.82 ZEC on April 14, 2026 and 7,320.535 ZEC on October 16, 2026, the last through the shielded pool, so only the total is visible there. Fees aside, the sheet and the chain agree to the zatoshi.

Approval shares on the page are of the ZEC voted for or against; abstentions are shown beside them. Zakura shows the leading answer's share of all ZEC, abstentions included; both come from the same tallies. The "asked for a lower amount" line appears under a question when that option drew at least 5% of the votes for or against.

`/api/zcg/coinholder/round` keeps its fields and adds `programName`, `administrator`, `page`, `round.status`, `round.resultsUrl`, `round.talliesSource`, `round.turnout`, `round.nextRound`, `payouts` (totals and one row per paid round: `paidOn`, `grants`, `usd`, `zec`, the implied `zecUsd` and FPF's `source`, plus `pendingRound`) and, per proposal, `outcome` and `tallyZec`. Zarvis reads it for coinholder questions.

## Verify it yourself

`src/lib/coinholder/verify.ts` holds the checks, as pure functions with tests:

- `applyZip1016(tally)`: a question passes with at least 420,000 ZEC voted on it and more ZEC for Accept than for Reject and Reject at a lower amount together. Abstentions are left out of the majority: str4d's reading, which FPF accepted on 2026-09-30 (thread 57909, posts 3 and 4). ZIP 1016 does not say whether Abstain counts toward the 420,000 ZEC minimum, and no published source settles it (str4d's post leaves it out of both; FPF's reply only confirms the change): the page counts it (`quorumMet`, as the Results page does) and keeps the other reading beside it (`quorumMetWithoutAbstain`, `turnsOnAbstain`). No outcome of any round turns on it; in Q1 2026 ZChat and zcash.me fall under the minimum without Abstain and were rejected either way, which the Q1 card says. `ZIP_1016_RULE.confirmedBy` cites FPF's reply for the majority only.
- `parseVoteSummary(json)`: the vote chain's `/shielded-vote/v1/vote-summary/<round>` JSON as ZEC per option and ballot number. `total_value` counts units of 0.125 ZEC (divided by 8) and a zero field is omitted (`total_value`, `ballot_count`, `index`). Options are classified by their wording, not their position. Anything malformed throws. `ballot_count` is dropped on purpose: it counts revealed encrypted shares, not voters, and is never shown as a voter count.
- `verifyRound(round, live?)`: one row per question with the four options, total, quorum, Accept's share of the ZEC for or against, the rule outcome (Funded or Rejected; Passed or Not passed for the ratification poll), the published outcome and whether they agree. A vetoed grant agrees (it passed the vote; the veto came later), a corrected result carries its correction (Connaugh Zcash Videos: first published as Abstain), a discarded question is not counted. With a live summary, `matchesChain` compares all four options exactly, matched by ballot number, never by title.

`src/lib/coinholder/vote-chain.ts` fetches the live summary (server only): Valar Group's primary first, the Zcash Foundation mirror as soon as Valar fails or has not answered within 1.5 seconds, the first good answer winning and the other request cancelled; one 5-second deadline for the whole check; the body must name the round asked for (its base64 `vote_round_id`); SHA-256 of the raw body; cached in-process for an hour, a failure retried after five minutes while the last good copy is served. It never throws: the page then says the chain is unavailable and shows the stored tallies. `verify-sources.ts` holds the servers, the vote-chain round per program round, the page's commands and how each Coin Voting round is recounted (Q1 2026: a single election covering all seven questions, recounted with the Voting Authorities' counter, hhanh00/zcv, built with `cargo build --release --bin counter --features tally`, and the published election seed, thread 55048 post 17; Q4 2025 and the ratification poll: Coin Voting 2.0's audit tool, hhanh00/zcash-vote-audit, with the seed and URL published for each question).

**API.** `GET /api/coinholders/verify?round=<id>&format=json|csv` (default: the latest round with a vote, JSON). JSON carries the round, the rule, the counts, `chain` (host, URL, SHA-256, bytes, fetch time, or why it was unavailable; null for Coin Voting rounds), how the round is recounted, its sources, the script URL and `rows` (`ballot`, `title`, `accept`, `reject`, `lower`, `abstain`, `total`, `quorumMet`, `quorumMetWithoutAbstain`, `approveShare`, `rule`, `published`, `agrees`, `note`, `matchesChain`); `rule` says which reading of the minimum it applies (`quorumCountsAbstain`) and what FPF's reply covers. CSV has the same rows at the precision the source published (3 decimals for Q3 2026, 2 for Q1 2026, whole ZEC before). `Content-Disposition` names the file `coinholder-<round>-verify.<ext>` (inline for JSON, attachment for CSV); cached 10 minutes, one minute when the chain did not answer. An unknown round, or one without a vote, is a 404 in JSON that lists the valid ids; an unknown format is a 400.

**Script.** `public/verify/coinholder_verify.py` (served as text/plain so it can be read before it is run): standard-library Python 3 that fetches a finalized summary (Zcash Foundation mirror by default, `--host valar`), checks that the body names the round asked for (its base64 `vote_round_id`), divides by 8, applies ZIP 1016, prints a CSV to stdout and the SHA-256 to stderr, and with `--compare <our JSON URL>` exits non-zero if any ballot's four options, total, quorum, rule outcome or published outcome differ, or if our JSON has a ballot the chain does not. `verify-commands.test.ts` runs the page's jq commands and the script against `fixtures/q3-2026-vote-summary.json`, a byte-exact copy of the finalized Q3 summary (SHA-256 `9b858326…6639b5`), when jq and python3 are installed.

**What it does not check yet.** Re-deriving the finalized summary from the encrypted shares in the vote chain's blocks: the page trusts the summary the chain's servers publish and shows its hash. For Coin Voting rounds the table is the site's copy of the published totals, not re-read from the posts; the seeds let anyone recount the ballots with the tools linked. The page's "Checked here" list follows the check itself: the vote-chain line appears only when the comparison ran.

**Page.** `src/lib/coinholder/verify-view.ts` computes every word and figure the page shows from the check itself, with tests: the verdict (`verdictOf`: a result checks out when the rule gives the published outcome and, where compared, its tally equals the chain's; `verdictChecks` for the row of three), the chart's rows (`decisionRows`, `sortDecisions`, `chartText`; Yes/No for the ratification poll), each rebuild step's expected result (`expectations`: the hash shortened the same way as the verdict's chip, `9b858326…6639b5`, `37 rows × 4 options`, `17 Funded · 20 Rejected`, `compare: 37 of 37 agree` for Q3; the Fetch step also prints the full hash) and the spreadsheet formulas (`sheetFormulas`, column letters taken from `VERIFY_CSV_HEADER`; a test evaluates them on every row of the CSV). The guide's tab is remembered per browser in localStorage (`oz.coinholders.verify.path`); the Spreadsheet tab says it checks the rule on OpenZcash's numbers, while curl + jq and Python check the numbers against the chain. The Python tab's steps say what each does (Download the script, Run it, Compare), since the script fetches, converts and applies the rule in one run. Coin Voting rounds show their recount (`RECOUNTS`, each step with a `gist` and, where it has one, a `result`) without tabs; where a round has a vetoed grant, its Apply step says the rule counts it Funded and the veto came afterwards (`vetoNote`). The chart and the table name the measure the same way, "Accept share" ("Yes share" for the poll); chart names wrap rather than truncate, and below 820px of column each label sits above its bar.

**Table.** Folded under "All figures (N proposals)", below the guide. Wide screens get one row per question; below the table's width (760px of container, or 1,080px for a four-option ballot such as Q3 2026) each question becomes a card with six figures to a line, and three on phones. `Accept share` never rounds to 100.0% or 0.0% while the other side has ZEC (">99.9%", "<0.1%"); a key above the table says what each column means.

When a round closes on the vote chain, add its chain round id to `VOTE_CHAIN_ROUNDS`; a Coin Voting round needs an entry in `RECOUNTS`.

## Value of the vote

Each round stores `closePrice`: the Bitfinex ZEC/USD daily close (UTC) on the day voting ended (`src/data/funding/days.json` holds the same series; a test checks every round against it). `voteValue()` in `derive.ts` values the largest single vote at that close and at the live quote (`currentZecUsdQuote`, so `/coinholders` renders dynamically), and sets the requested amount against it. How many holders voted is not published for any round, and the shielded vote does not reveal it: ballot counts measure submissions (and on the Q3 vote chain, revealed encrypted shares), so the page uses ZEC voted only.

## Official outcomes on the FPF and ZCG pages

The official Coinholder sheet lags the results post, and the site publishes status changes the next day (Brasília time). `src/lib/coinholder/official-status.ts` overlays the curated outcomes from `ROUNDS` on coinholder rows, matched by the FPF GitHub issue number in the row's link: proposals in a closed round show Approved, Rejected or Keyholder veto, and approved grants the sheet still marks Open show **Awaiting payment** while `PAYOUTS` has their round as `approved`. Once a round is `paid`, every row keeps the ledger's status and `awaitingGrantsMissingFrom` is empty (as it is now: every closed round is paid). `createOfficialStatus(rounds, payouts)` builds the overlay for a given set of rounds and payouts; the live one uses `ROUNDS` and `PAYOUTS`, and the tests keep a fixture with Q3 2026 pending. It is used on `/zcg/fpf`, `/zcg/proposals`, `/zcg/grants` and `/zcg/grant`; proposals outside a closed round keep the database status. The raw data API and Zarvis still read the sheet as imported.

## When a round closes

1. Add the round at the top of `ROUNDS` (it becomes the default tab of `/coinholders/results`) with outcomes from the results post and tallies from the vote chain, and move the previous round down.
2. Give its `windows` `start`/`end` dates (the stepper counts days from them) and its `closePrice` (Bitfinex daily close on the day voting ended). Update `PROGRAM.nextRound` from the results post, the pending entry in `PAYOUTS` (the fund page reads it) and the round's entry in `src/data/governance/record.json`.
3. `npx vitest run src/lib/coinholder` checks counts, requested totals, the 0.125 ZEC grid, the published turnout range and that every outcome agrees with ZIP 1016 as applied; extend it with the new round's figures.
4. Register the round for `/coinholders/verify`: its vote-chain round id in `VOTE_CHAIN_ROUNDS` (verify-sources.ts), or its recount in `RECOUNTS` for another voting system.

## When a round is paid

1. In `PAYOUTS`, set the round's `status` to `paid`, its `date` and its `zec` (the sum of the round's `ZEC Disbursed` cells in the Coinholder sheet), and point `source` at FPF's confirmation. `fund.test.ts` checks the totals and that each paid round's USD equals its funded grants.
2. Replace the round's "awaiting payment" note with the payment (date, ZEC, price, how it was sent), add FPF's confirmation, the sheet and the multisig to its `sources`, and give each funded proposal a "Paid …" note with its ZEC.
3. Add the payout to the fund page's story and run `npm run knowledge:build` so Zarvis reads the new text.
